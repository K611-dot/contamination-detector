Australia is a country and continent in the southern hemisphere. Its
largest cities include Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane and Perth. The
country is known for its distinctive wildlife, including kangaroos,
koalas and the platypus, and for a vast arid interior often referred to
as the outback.

Coral reefs are underwater ecosystems built by colonies of tiny animals
called polyps. They support enormous biodiversity but are vulnerable to
rising sea temperatures, which can trigger bleaching events where the
symbiotic algae living in coral tissue are expelled.

Cells are the basic structural unit of living organisms. Different
organelles inside a cell carry out specialised functions, from protein
synthesis to waste breakdown, coordinated by signals originating in the
nucleus.

Neural networks are a family of machine learning models loosely inspired
by biological neurons. They are trained by gradient descent on a loss
function, adjusting weights so predictions move closer to observed
targets over many passes through a training dataset.
